Types of AI agents we build

Lead-qualification agent

Engages inbound enquiries, asks the right questions, scores and routes the qualified ones to your team while filtering out the noise.

Customer-response agent

Handles routine customer messages across your channels, drafts accurate replies, and escalates to a human only when judgement is needed.

Operations agent

Runs multi-step internal workflows — moving data between systems, triggering tasks and keeping records in sync without manual handoffs.

Document / admin agent

Reads, extracts and processes documents — forms, invoices, contracts — turning hours of admin into a reviewed, structured output.

Internal-knowledge agent

Answers staff questions from your own documents, procedures and data — so institutional knowledge is searchable instead of locked in people's heads.

Voice / quote agent

Takes spoken input and turns it into structured results — for example a field worker dictating job details and getting a calculated quote back.

How an AI agent differs from a chatbot

A chatbot answers. It follows a script or pulls from a knowledge base and replies in the chat window — and that is where it stops. An AI agent acts. It can use tools, call your APIs, read and write records in your systems, make decisions and carry a multi-step task through to completion before handing back to a human.

That difference matters in practice. A chatbot can tell a customer how to request a quote; an agent can read the request, calculate the quote, draft it, log it in your CRM and notify the right person. The first deflects work back to your team; the second removes it. Building an agent well means giving it the right tools, the right guardrails and the right point to escalate — which is exactly what we scope in our AI opportunity audit before writing any code.
