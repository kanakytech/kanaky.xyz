What is the Kanaky Tech AI Opportunity Audit?

Kanaky Tech is a Pacific AI automation studio that offers a free AI opportunity audit — a structured review of a company's operations that identifies the highest-impact automation opportunities and gives you a snapshot of where to start. The audit is free and gives you direction and a preview; the full prioritised roadmap is the first paid deliverable, and the build is scoped separately after that. It is designed for SMEs, agencies and institutions across Kanaky (New Caledonia), New Zealand and the wider Pacific that want to know where AI fits before spending a dollar on it.

What the audit covers

Operations & admin

Repetitive back-office tasks — data entry, scheduling, approvals, internal hand-offs — where time disappears and AI agents can take the load off your team.

Customer & sales workflows

Lead intake, quoting, follow-ups, support replies and CRM updates — the front-of-house work that eats hours and quietly loses revenue when it slips.

Reporting & data

The reports nobody enjoys building — weekly summaries, dashboards, reconciliations — and the data trapped across spreadsheets and tools that could be assembled automatically.

Document handling

Invoices, contracts, forms and PDFs that are read, sorted, summarised or re-keyed by hand — prime territory for reliable, private document automation.

Tools & integrations

The gaps between the software you already pay for. We look at where systems don't talk to each other and where an agent can bridge them without a migration.

Team time-drains

The recurring "this takes me all morning" tasks your people mention but never get fixed. We surface them, size them, and rank them by how much they're costing you.

What you walk away with

A clear snapshot and a first move, not a sales pitch

The free audit ends with a concrete result — a snapshot of where AI fits your business and a clear recommendation on where to start. You keep it whether or not you work with us. Specifically, you receive:

  1. A shortlist of your top opportunities — the highest-impact automations we spotted, called out so the obvious first moves are obvious.
  2. A rough sense of time and cost saved — an honest, early read on what each opportunity could be worth based on how your team works today, not generic benchmarks.
  3. A clear recommendation on where to start — the single best first move, so you know exactly where to begin even before any paid work.
  4. Direction and a preview — the snapshot points the way; the full prioritised roadmap, with every opportunity analysed in depth, ROI per lever and a sequenced 30/60/90-day plan, is the first paid deliverable if you choose to go further.

If you'd rather see what this looks like in practice, our case studies walk through real production systems, and our business process automation page covers how we turn a roadmap into working tools.

02

Honest pricing

Free audit. Paid roadmap. Scoped build.

We keep the money side simple and transparent, because trust is the whole point of a first engagement. Three clear steps:

  • The AI opportunity audit is free. The discovery call, the review of how your business runs, a shortlist of your top opportunities, a rough sense of the savings and a clear recommendation on where to start — all at no cost and no obligation.
  • The full prioritised roadmap is the first paid deliverable. Every opportunity analysed in depth, ROI estimated per lever and a sequenced 30/60/90-day implementation plan — the detailed written document you act on.
  • The build is scoped separately. Once you've chosen what to pursue, we quote the implementation against that specific work — typically a quick-win sprint, then a wider rollout. No subscription required to start, no invented numbers.
