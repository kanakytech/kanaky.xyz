Where we come from

Five territories, thirteen million people, and almost nobody building for them.

Melanesia — New Caledonia, Papua New Guinea, Fiji, Vanuatu and Solomon Islands — has been a region in its own right since 1986, when these five formed the Melanesian Spearhead Group. Providers elsewhere tend to see either markets too small to bother with, or distant extensions of Australia.

We are from here, and we build accordingly: systems that keep working when the connection drops, data that stays on infrastructure you control, and pricing set against each market rather than one rate card written in Auckland.

In plain terms
What does Kanaky Tech automate?

Kanaky Tech automates the repetitive, manual work inside a business: data entry and admin, reporting and dashboards, lead qualification and follow-up, customer responses, quote and proposal drafting, and document and email processing. We do it with custom AI agents and workflow automation — keeping a human in the loop where judgement matters.

Who should contact Kanaky Tech?

Any SME, agency or institution losing hours to repetitive tasks, slow lead follow-up, manual reporting or document handling — and anyone who wants to use AI but doesn't know where to start. If your team is doing by hand what software could do for them, that's our starting point.

When should a company hire Kanaky Tech?

When growth is being capped by manual operations rather than demand; when good leads go cold because follow-up is slow; when reporting eats a full day every week; or when you want AI in your business but need someone to find the highest-impact place to start. The first step is always a free AI opportunity audit.

Where does Kanaky Tech operate?

Kanaky Tech operates from Nouméa (New Caledonia) and Auckland (New Zealand), serving businesses across the wider Pacific. Work is delivered remotely or on-site, in English or French.

About

Founder-led AI, built on the ground in Melanesia.

Kanaky Tech is an AI automation studio founded in New Caledonia in 2024 by Kevyn Wahuzue, an AI systems architect and digital entrepreneur. We sit at the intersection of practical AI, business operations and digital sovereignty.

Our approach is pragmatic and sovereignty-first: we build tools that serve local needs, run on infrastructure organisations control, and keep the value — and the data — in the region. We facilitate people; we don't replace them — unless that's exactly what you want.

Melanesia is our focus — New Caledonia, Papua New Guinea, Fiji, Vanuatu and Solomon Islands: five territories, around thirteen million people, joined since 1986 in the Melanesian Spearhead Group. We price per market rather than applying one rate card, because purchasing power varies fifteen-fold across the region. We also work across New Zealand, Australia and the wider Pacific, with systems already live in production.
