What we automate for NC businesses

If a task in your business is repetitive, rule-based and eats hours every week, it is usually a candidate for automation. These are the capabilities we deploy most often for companies in New Caledonia — generic on purpose, because the right starting point depends on your workflows. The AI audit tells us which one to build first.

Admin & devis / quotes

Generate quotes, invoices and recurring admin documents from a few inputs instead of rebuilding them by hand each time.

Customer response

First-line replies to common customer messages and enquiries, drafted or sent automatically, in French and English.

Reporting

Pull numbers from your tools and assemble recurring reports — weekly, monthly, board-level — without manual copy-paste.

Lead qualification

Score, sort and route incoming leads so your team spends time on the prospects worth chasing, not on triage.

Document processing

Read, sort and extract data from PDFs, forms and emails — turning unstructured documents into clean, usable records.

Custom AI agents

Beyond single tasks, we build AI agents that chain several steps together and act on your behalf — see AI agents in Nouméa.
