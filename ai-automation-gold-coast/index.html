Three practical starting points

Trades: prepare a quote without losing the evening

A plumber or builder receives a voice note, photos and a job address. A workflow can gather these into a job record, flag missing details and prepare a draft from an approved price list. The owner checks scope, travel, materials and margin before sending. Success means less preparation time per quote without more corrections. See AI for builders.

Marine: organise service enquiries before booking work

A boat owner supplies a vessel model, engine details and a fault description across several messages. A workflow can consolidate the record, request missing information and route it to the right person. A technician makes the diagnosis and confirms parts and availability. This is an intake workflow, not an autonomous safety assessment. See AI for marine businesses.

Tourism and hospitality: answer accurately, then hand over

An accommodation or activity operator can draft answers from its approved policies and booking data. The system should hand over exceptions, refunds and uncertain answers. Weather-dependent activities need an operator decision; an AI response must not invent availability or confirm a departure without authorisation. See AI for hospitality.

These are proposed workflow designs. They are not claims about completed Gold Coast client projects or guaranteed savings.

What the first engagement includes

  1. A free remote audit: map one process, the tools involved and the time currently spent.
  2. A written scope: inputs, outputs, human approvals, integrations, acceptance criteria and a fixed project quote.
  3. A pilot using agreed examples, including missing information, duplicate requests and service outages.
  4. A documented handover: how to run it, correct a mistake, pause it and take ownership. Support and recurring provider costs are agreed separately.

Budget and return: make the calculation visible

The quote depends on the integrations, data preparation, review requirements and deployment environment. Confirm the currency, any applicable tax, setup fees, hosting, AI usage and support before committing. Existing software may already solve the problem; the audit should identify that too.

For illustration only: 40 requests a week with six minutes of net handling time saved per request would release four hours a week. Measure net time after human checking and corrections. This is a calculation to test in a pilot, not a forecast of your result.

Working together across the Tasman

Delivery, training and support are remote. Gold Coast uses Queensland time; Auckland is two or three hours ahead depending on the season. Meeting invitations specify the time zone and date. If your project requires on-site hardware installation, agree a local delivery arrangement before proceeding.

Data handling and sources to check

Before connecting live customer data, document which fields leave each system, which providers receive them, where they are processed, who has access and how they are deleted. An Australian hosting region alone does not establish that every integration stays in Australia. Use the OAIC guidance on AI products to frame the privacy review. For outbound messaging, check ACMA guidance on avoiding spam before enabling a campaign.

Common questions

Is Kanaky Tech based on the Gold Coast?

No. Kanaky Tech is based in Auckland, Aotearoa, and serves Gold Coast businesses remotely. There is no Gold Coast office.

Can you automate quoting and booking enquiries?

Yes, subject to the tools and data available. A pilot can collect information, draft responses and prepare records. Pricing decisions, exceptions and final commitments remain with the authorised person unless a specific approval policy is agreed.

How much does a project cost?

The initial audit is free. Implementation is quoted against an agreed scope. The proposal separates setup from recurring hosting, AI usage and support, and states the currency and applicable tax.

Can we keep our existing business software?

Usually the aim is to connect existing tools. API access, permissions and export capabilities are checked during scoping. We identify limitations before committing to an integration.
