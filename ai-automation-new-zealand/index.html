What is Kanaky Tech?

Kanaky Tech is an AI automation agency operating from Auckland (and Nouméa), founded in 2024, helping New Zealand SMEs and agencies automate manual business processes and deploy custom AI agents. We are private-by-default and available under NDA, and we are registered in New Zealand (NZBN 9429053554017). The model is simple: a free AI audit first, then a fast, scoped quick-win so you see value before committing to anything larger.

Most New Zealand businesses do not have an AI problem — they have a repetition problem. Quotes typed out by hand, the same onboarding emails every week, reports rebuilt from scratch each month, leads triaged manually. Each task is small; together they swallow days. As an AI automation agency in Auckland, our job is to find that work and let software do it, so your people spend their time on the things only people can do.

We are deliberately a business automation NZ partner, not a software vendor selling licences. We start from your actual workflows, build automation around the tools you already use, and frame everything around payback. If you are looking for an AI consultant in Auckland who ships working systems rather than slide decks, that is the gap we fill — and we do it the same way across New Caledonia and the wider Pacific.

What we automate for NZ businesses

Operations & admin

The recurring back-office work that quietly eats hours — data moved between tools, statuses updated, follow-ups sent. We automate the loop so it runs itself.

Client onboarding

New client signs — and a chain of emails, forms, account setup and intro docs fires automatically. Consistent every time, with no one chasing the checklist.

Reporting

Monthly and weekly reports pulled together from your tools and written up automatically — the same numbers, the same format, without the rebuild-from-scratch ritual.

Lead qualification

Inbound enquiries scored, sorted and routed automatically, so your team talks to the right people first instead of sifting the inbox.

Proposal & quote generation

Quotes and proposals drafted from a few inputs — accurate calculations, your wording, your pricing — turning a 30-minute job into a 30-second one.

Internal knowledge

Scattered docs, SOPs and past projects turned into a private AI knowledge base your team can simply ask — answers in seconds, not a search through folders.

These are the patterns we see again and again across New Zealand SMEs and agencies. The right starting point is rarely the most exciting one — it is the most repetitive one. Our free AI audit exists to find it, and our business process automation work turns it into a system that runs without you.
