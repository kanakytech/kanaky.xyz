When to reach out

When should a company contact Kanaky Tech?

You don't need a finished AI strategy to get in touch. Most teams that contact Kanaky Tech are simply spending too many hours on work a machine could do, and want a clear, honest read on what is worth automating before they commit a budget. If any of the following sound like your week, it is the right time to reach out.

  • You are drowning in admin — quotes, invoices, scheduling and data entry are eating hours every day.
  • Reporting is still manual — someone rebuilds the same spreadsheet or report by hand every week or month.
  • Lead response is slow — enquiries sit unanswered because no one has time to reply or qualify them fast.
  • Repetitive document work piles up — reading, sorting and extracting data from PDFs, forms and emails by hand.
  • You want to use AI but don't know where to start — you sense the opportunity but need someone to map it without the hype.

In each case the next step is the same: a free AI opportunity audit that names the highest-return tasks first. From there, Kanaky Tech can build business process automation or a custom AI agent — quoted per project, scoped before any build.
