Our story

Technology becomes meaningful when it starts from the ground.

Kanaky Tech was born in 2024 in Kanaky–New Caledonia, then grew between Nouméa and Auckland. It began with a simple belief: the Pacific should not only consume tools designed elsewhere. It can build its own.

Our projects became proof of that vision: preserve languages, make cultural sources verifiable, open public data to AI agents, and give businesses systems they can understand and control.

Our vision

Useful here. Open when possible. Always controllable.
