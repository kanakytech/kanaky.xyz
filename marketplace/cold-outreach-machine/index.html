It writes a different email for every single one

Not a template with the name swapped out. The machine reads what each company actually publishes — what they build, what they promise, what they're proud of — and writes one email about them. The person who receives it can't tell it was automated, because in every way that matters, it wasn't.

A verification script compares every message against every other one. If any two share a six-word sequence, the batch is rejected and rewritten. That's the test our own campaign passes before a single email leaves.

03

It sends them without burning your domain

This is where most outreach tools quietly kill your business. Blast a hundred emails in an hour and your domain gets classified as bulk — after that nothing you send reaches anyone, including your invoices. The machine spaces every message 25 to 45 minutes apart, sends only during business hours, and stops on its own when the day's quota is reached.

It also watches for replies and tells you the moment a real person answers. Bounces and auto-responders are filtered out, so you're only interrupted when it matters.

Questions

What people ask before buying.

Do I need to be technical? +
You need to be able to follow instructions and copy-paste — that's genuinely it. The installation is one command that opens a guided menu and configures everything by asking you questions, the documentation assumes no programming knowledge, and the setup call exists precisely so the technical part is done with you rather than left to you. If you can set up an email account, you can run this.
Can I run it without sending my data to an AI company? +
Yes — and it's a one-line change, not a different product. The machine talks to any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, so you have two options. Cloud provider: best writing quality, costs a few cents per email, and your lead list passes through their servers. Local model: point it at a model running on your own computer instead — no per-email cost ever, and your prospect data never leaves your machine, which matters if your client list is the business. The trade-off is honest: a local model needs a reasonably capable computer (16GB of memory or more), runs slower, and writes slightly less sharply than the large cloud models. Most people start on the cloud and move local once volume makes the cost add up. We configure whichever you want during the setup call.
Isn't this just spam? +
Spam is one message sent to thousands of people who were never considered. This is the opposite: a small number of companies chosen because they fit, each one researched, each email written about them specifically, sent once and never repeated. The machine keeps a permanent exclusion list and will not contact anyone who has asked to be left alone. That's the difference — and it's why the replies are conversations rather than complaints.
What actually stops my domain getting blacklisted? +
Three things, built in rather than optional. Messages are spaced 25 to 45 minutes apart instead of sent in bursts. Sending happens only inside business hours, because a 3am email is a bot signature. And a hard daily ceiling is enforced that the machine won't cross even if you tell it to. The documentation also walks you through SPF, DKIM and DMARC — which is what actually gets you into inboxes.
Can I use it for more than one business? +
Yes. The licence covers you, for as many of your own businesses and markets as you like, forever. What it doesn't allow is reselling the code itself or running it as a service for third parties — that needs a separate agreement, and we're happy to talk if that's what you have in mind.
How fast will I see replies? +
Honestly, it depends on your market, your offer, and how well you describe your ideal customer. In our own first week we had a meeting booked with the owner of three marinas within three days, and a detailed reply from a boatbuilder a few days later. We won't promise you the same — anyone who does is guessing. What we can promise is that the emails get written, get delivered, and get read.
What if it doesn't work for me? +
If the machine doesn't run after we've done the setup call together, we refund you in full. That's a promise about the product doing what it says. We can't refund based on how many people reply to your offer, because that depends on the offer — and that part was always yours.
