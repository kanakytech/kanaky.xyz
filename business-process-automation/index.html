What is Kanaky Tech business process automation?

Kanaky Tech is a Pacific business-process-automation studio that maps a company's manual workflows and replaces the repetitive parts with AI agents and automations — admin, ops, reporting, lead qualification, document processing — so teams focus on higher-value work. Founded 2024; operates from Kanaky (New Caledonia) and New Zealand.

What we automate

Admin & data entry

The copy-paste between spreadsheets, forms and tools. Records created, updated and synced automatically instead of by hand.

Reporting & dashboards

Recurring reports pulled, formatted and delivered on schedule — weekly numbers, ops summaries, live dashboards — without anyone rebuilding them every time.

Lead qualification & follow-up

Inbound enquiries scored, routed and followed up automatically, so no lead goes cold while your team is busy doing the real work.

Document & email processing

PDFs, forms and inboxes read, sorted and extracted — invoices, contracts, applications — turned into structured data and the right next action.

Customer response

First-line replies drafted or sent automatically — FAQs, status updates, routine messages — with your team handling only what genuinely needs them.

Quote / proposal generation

Quotes, devis and proposals assembled from your inputs in minutes — priced, formatted and ready to send — instead of rebuilt from scratch each time.

Our philosophy

Augment humans, don't replace them.

Automation done badly tries to remove people. We do the opposite. The point of automating a process is to strip out the parts a person hates anyway — the chasing, the copy-paste, the formatting, the manual re-keying — so the human is freed to do the work that actually needs them: judgement, relationships, decisions, care.

Every system we build is designed to facilitate people, not cut them. A good automation makes a small team feel larger, a stretched team feel calmer, and a skilled person spend their day on skilled work. If the only "win" of a project is fewer staff, we think it was scoped wrong. That principle also shapes how we treat your data — see our private AI systems approach, where business data runs in controlled environments and is never used to train public models.

How it works
01 — Audit

Audit

We map your real workflows and find where the manual hours go. The output is a ranked list of what's worth automating first. This is the free AI audit.

02 — Design

Design

We design the automation around how your team actually works — the inputs, the edge cases, the hand-offs — and agree a clear scope before anything is built.

03 — Build

Build

We build with AI agents, N8N, your existing APIs and MCP. Tested against real cases, not demos, so it survives the messy inputs of day-to-day operations.

04 — Embed

Embed

We deploy it into your workflow and make sure the team adopts it. We track hours saved and errors removed, then tune. The win has to be real and observed.

FAQ

Business process automation is the practice of taking the repetitive, rule-based work inside a company — data entry, reporting, lead follow-up, document handling — and having software do it instead of people. With modern AI, automation can also handle messier tasks like reading PDFs, drafting replies or qualifying leads. The goal is to remove the manual grind so your team spends its hours on judgement, relationships and higher-value work.

Start with the tasks that are repetitive, rule-based and eat hours every week — that combination gives the fastest payback. Common first targets are recurring reports, quote and proposal generation, first-line customer responses, lead qualification and document processing.
