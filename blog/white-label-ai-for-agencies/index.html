White-label AI production, delivered under your brand

Creative and digital agencies are winning AI and automation pitches. The hard part comes next: actually shipping the work. Kanaky Tech is the quiet production and automation arm that delivers the last mile — visuals, video, workflows and custom agents — as if it were in-house, under your name, under NDA.

By Kevyn WahuzueKanaky Tech · Auckland & NouméaJune 29, 2026
What does a white-label AI production partner do for an agency?

A white-label AI production partner builds and ships the AI work an agency has sold — but entirely under the agency's own brand. Concretely, Kanaky Tech delivers three things for agencies: AI visual and video production at volume (product shots, ad creative, social sets, motion), workflow automation that removes manual steps, and custom AI agents grounded in real data. We work under NDA, we never contact your clients, and our name appears nowhere in the deliverables. You keep the relationship, the margin and the credit. We just make sure the thing you promised in the pitch actually gets delivered.

The pitch is won. Now what?

Here is a pattern we see again and again with agencies in Auckland, Wellington and across the Pacific. The team is sharp, the client trusts them, and they close the AI or automation pitch — "we'll generate your whole product catalogue with AI", "we'll automate your reporting", "we'll build you an assistant". Everyone shakes hands.

Then the last mile hits. The demo that wowed the client in the room turns out to be a long way from a finished, on-brand, client-ready deliverable. The tools are fiddly, the outputs are inconsistent, the automation breaks on edge cases, and the one person on the team who "knows AI" is already overloaded. The gap between the demo and the delivery is where good agencies quietly lose money, miss deadlines, or hand back a result they're not proud of.

That gap is exactly what a white-label AI production partner exists to close.

What "white-label" actually means here

White-label is a word that gets thrown around, so let's be precise about what we mean. When we say we deliver under your brand, we mean:

  • Your name on everything. Deliverables, file names, presentation, tone — all yours. We adapt to your brand and your house style, not the other way around.
  • We don't exist to your client. We don't email them, we don't join their calls unless you want a silent technical hand, and we never publish their project as our case study without written permission.
  • NDA by default. Confidentiality isn't an upsell. It's how the whole arrangement works. Discretion is the product.

In short: you look like you have a serious in-house AI production capability, because functionally you do. It's ours, and it answers to you.

What we deliver

We deliberately keep the offer to what we can ship reliably and at quality. Three lanes:

1. AI visual & video production

This is the highest-volume need. Product shots, lifestyle scenes, ad creative, full social sets, story and reel covers, short motion clips — produced with AI pipelines, then cleaned up and made genuinely usable. The honest selling point is volume and speed: tens of on-brand visuals in the time a traditional shoot would take to schedule. We handle the messy part — consistency, brand fit, the dozen rejected variants nobody sees — so what lands in your client's inbox just works.

2. Workflow automation

Less glamorous, often more valuable. We map a repetitive process — reporting, content repurposing, lead handling, data entry, file shuffling — and automate it end to end, so a task that ate hours a week runs on its own. Sometimes it's for your client's operations; sometimes it's for yours, to give your team back the hours the AI pitch was supposed to free up in the first place.

3. Custom AI agents

Assistants, internal tools and chatbots grounded in real data rather than the open web. The principle we hold to is the same one behind our private AI systems: the agent answers from your verified content, so it doesn't confidently make things up. A support assistant trained on the client's actual documentation. An internal tool that turns plain questions into answers from their own database. Built to be reliable, not just impressive in a demo.

Relationships, not volume

We're upfront about how we want to work: we'd rather build a handful of real, long-running relationships with agencies than churn through one-off jobs. That shapes everything. We say no to work we can't deliver well. We give you an honest read at the audit stage instead of selling you a project that's going to disappoint your client. And when something goes sideways — because sometimes it does — we'd rather fix it openly than protect a margin.

This is the same posture we take on every engagement, whether it's a Kanak heritage project or a commercial build: reliable, private, useful, and grounded. If you want to see how that translates into finished work, our case studies are the honest version, not a highlight reel.
