The problem: visual production is slow and expensive

Every agency that produces visuals for product, e-commerce or social clients runs into the same wall. A single commercial visual — a clean product shot, a lifestyle scene, a variant for a marketplace listing — costs on the order of $18 and about 25 minutes of skilled work between shooting, retouching and adapting. Scale that to a real brief and the maths gets ugly fast: a 50-visual product campaign is roughly 25 hours of work, before a single video or voiceover is even discussed.

That cost structure does two things to an agency. It caps how many briefs it can take on, and it squeezes margins on the briefs it does take. The bottleneck isn't talent — it's the slow, manual production line behind the talent.

What Keou Studio does

Keou Studio collapses that production line. You upload a product photo and generate commercial-grade visuals, videos, voice-overs and format variants in minutes, at the cost of the underlying AI API — on the order of $0.09 per image rather than $18.

This is AI product photography treated as infrastructure, not a toy — and the platform itself costs nothing. It is free because the compute is yours: you paste your own generation key, it never leaves your browser, and you pay your provider directly. Kanaky Tech takes no cut and adds no platform fee.

The end-to-end workflow

Keou Studio is a complete AI creative production platform, not a single generator. The workflow runs from a source image all the way to a client sign-off — and every creative feature below is in the free platform:

  • Production studio. Batch product photography from a single source image — lifestyle scenes, studio packshots and seasonal campaigns, generated in parallel.
  • Polish, remix & adapt. Retouch a render, re-imagine it in a new direction, or adapt it to any aspect ratio — without starting over.
  • Cinematic video. Turn any render into motion — drift shots, product B-roll, social clips, with sound.
  • Clients, campaigns & library. Organise work per client and campaign and browse your full generation history.
  • 30-format delivery packs. One click exports a campaign-ready pack — stories, feeds, banners, covers — named, sized and zipped for hand-off.
  • Voice & sound. Text-to-speech voice-overs and generated sound effects, plus 4K upscaling for stills and video.
  • Client share links. Send a clean review page where your client approves, rejects or comments on each asset — with no account needed. Just a secure link.

The distinctive hook: the platform takes no cut

Most creative tools price per seat or per generation, so the tool gets more expensive exactly as you get more productive. Keou Studio inverts that: you pay your AI provider and nothing else. A typical product visual costs a few cents of provider credit, and the platform adds zero — forever, on every feature. There is no seat count to manage, no trial clock, and nothing to cancel.
